What is Print on Demand?<\/span><\/p>\n

Print on demand (Print on Demand or POD)<\/span> is a business model in which printed products, such as books, t-shirts, mugs, and other personalized items, are produced only when a customer requests them. Instead of printing large quantities in advance and keeping inventory, the items are printed and manufactured individually after a purchase is made.<\/span><\/p>\n

How It Works?<\/span><\/p>\n

In my case, print-on-demand works as follows: I create the designs, but I don't have my own suppliers for printing.<\/span><\/p>\n

Additionally, if you prefer, you can also request custom designs and take them to any place that offers printing services so they can print them to your liking in the format you prefer. This way, you have the flexibility to choose how and where to get your products!<\/span><\/p>\n\n\n
